# Textbook III — Intellectual Property, Open Source, and AI Assets

**Edition:** 2026-11-11. Original educational writing; United States orientation. Not legal advice.

## Course outcomes
Distinguish copyright, patent, trademark, trade secret and contract rights; analyze copying with the correct statutory elements; inventory code, model weights, datasets, game art, fonts and music independently; explain license obligations on commercial distribution or network use; recognize limitations of AI-output copyright and anti-circumvention exceptions.
