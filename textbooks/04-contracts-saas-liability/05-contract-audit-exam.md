# Chapter 5 — Contract and subscription lab

## Capstone: launch an AI writing SaaS
**Hypothetical facts:** Free seven-day trial followed by $29/month; enterprise MSA with 99.9% SLA; a pilot promotion with a conflicting forum clause; provider logs prompts for 30 days; marketing says “zero retention”; a customer complains about hard-to-find cancellation.

### Tasks
1. **Formation (20 pts):** Produce a mock checkout/assent sequence; record versions and UI proof, identify governing state law unknowns.
2. **Conflicts (20):** Write a *Coinbase v. Suski* analysis of arbitration versus promotional forum clause; do not assume one always supersedes.
3. **Subscriptions (20):** Analyze ROSCA §8403 and state-law issues, explicitly rejecting reliance on vacated 2024 FTC click-to-cancel amendments.
4. **AI provider (20):** Create data processing/claims matrix identifying the mismatch between “zero retention” and 30-day logging and remediation.
5. **Liability (20):** Discuss contractual cap/indemnity carveouts, FTC §5, breach duties and uncertain facts.

## Model answer outline
**Formation:** actual displayed assent, jurisdiction, consideration and terms controls need evidence.  
**Conflicts:** court decides antecedent conflict over who decides arbitrability where two agreements allocate it differently, per *Coinbase*; ultimate dispute outcome separate.  
**Subscriptions:** show material billing terms before payment details; express informed consent and simple cancellation mechanism as ROSCA requires for covered transactions.  
**Claims:** revise contradictory representation and/or actual vendor architecture.  
**Liability:** do not claim that a generic cap or warranty disclaimer defeats all statutory claims.
