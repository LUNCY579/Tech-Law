# Textbook VII — International Technology Law and Cross-Border Commercialization

**Edition:** 2026-11-11 · **Status:** Original educational study material, not legal/tax advice or regulatory clearance.

This volume is written for a U.S.-based AI/SaaS founder considering customers, employers, hosting, model services or distributors in Europe and other jurisdictions. **A product being accessible online does not by itself answer territorial jurisdiction; moving data is not the same as exporting controlled technology; registering a company is not the same as satisfying VAT or consumer rules.**

## Research and writing rules

Use the **actual legal text** (statute or regulation), official court opinion, or official agency implementation guidance; label each accurately. References to the European Commission or U.S. government do not constitute permission to mirror every image, PDF, logo or third-party component. All lessons contain original explanations; authoritative legal documents are **linked rather than reproduced**.

For an actual product launch, always confirm effective text and amendments, entity/business facts, applicable jurisdictions and relevant counsel before concluding compliance.

## Founder's five questions

1. **Who sells what to whom, where, and in which role?**
2. **What personal data move across which borders, including through vendors and remote support?**
3. **Do AI-specific or platform-specific obligations apply to this function?**
4. **Are any people, destinations, end uses, software, weights or technical disclosures restricted?**
5. **Who collects tax, fulfills cancellation rights, bears liability and responds to regulator requests?**

An affirmative answer to one is not a substitute for the other four.
