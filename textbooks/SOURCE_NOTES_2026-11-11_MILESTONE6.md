## Scope and permissions
Only original lesson prose, original invented examples and brief case explanations were added. Statutes, official court opinions and regulator pages are **linked**, not reproduced. Official availability alone is not a universal license for every embedded third-party work. The EEOC and SEC commentary are attributed as guidance; their regulatory requirements are not assumed to apply to every technology startup.

## Residual risk / open work
1. Exact effective versions and applicable state data-breach statutes must be individually verified for live incidents.
2. Employment litigation holdings (*Griggs* and related cases), current case-history and lower-court interpretations still need primary opinion page verification.
