# Chapter 4 — Credential provenance, independent review and recruiter rights

## Separate the four assertions
1. **Identity verified:** identity proofing decision was completed under a specified process.
2. **Attempt verified:** evidence proves a named candidate completed a specific assessment attempt with defined controls.
3. **Score authenticated:** cryptographic signature binds form, rubric, raw scoring evidence and release version.
4. **Assessment scientifically validated:** independent study demonstrates the score supports the *claimed inference* for a specified population/role.

A badge should never conflate these categories. Describing a pilot instrument as “scientifically certified” without support can create misleading representations and affect employment decisions.

## Candidate/employer consent and privacy
Protect private evidence using subject–action–resource and purpose restrictions. Candidate can grant/revoke employer access under a current permission version; revoke stale sessions, evidence links and queued introductions. Evaluate GDPR, California law and contractual requirements separately by geography and role, rather than asserting all users have a single worldwide statutory right.

## Synthetic platform review
- Candidate A and B; Employer X and Y.
- Employer X can see only A's expressly granted evidence.
- After A revokes, the grant cannot be resurrected from stale tabs, pending queues or delayed caches.
- Employer X cannot approve itself as “verified”; independent authorization and audit trails are necessary.
- Historical credentials may display “revoked,” “expired,” “unvalidated pilot” or “self reported” distinctly.

## Employment-adjacent legal issues
Before employers rely on rankings: analyze [42 U.S.C. §2000e-2](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t42/s2000e-2/) and [§12112](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/119-120/t42/s12112/); relevant state/local AI employment rules and privacy laws may apply. The authentication of evidence is **not equivalent** to lawful selection procedure validation.

## Exercise
An applicant owns a verified credential but withdraws public publication. Employer still possesses an old screenshot. **Answer:** Revoke live platform access as applicable; distinguish already obtained copies, contractual restrictions, and retention/legal-hold issues. Don't falsely promise technological erasure of every external copy.


## Milestone 20: automated selection versus credential proof

A signed job-assessment score is evidence of **issuer-authenticated provenance**, not evidence that an employer complied with hiring law. The [Illinois and NYC automated hiring lesson](../../research/2026-11-13/14-hiring-ai-video-and-aedt-2026.md) distinguishes **Illinois video-interview consent/deletion (820 ILCS 42)**, **Illinois anti-discrimination and AI employee notice (775 ILCS 5/2-102(L))**, and **NYC Local Law 144 AEDT employer bias-audit/publication/notice obligations**. A vendor’s self-produced “fairness certificate” does not substitute for an independent audit where required, nor establish Title VII or ADA validation. Preserve score, employer decision-use facts, source versions, independent-auditor provenance and all unresolved exceptions.
