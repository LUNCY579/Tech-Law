# Employment AI assessment examination (100 points)

**Fact pattern:** A recruitment SaaS produces “scientifically validated” coding aptitude scores using one uncalibrated 20-item test. It rejects applicants below 60 automatically, provides only a mouse-based interface and voice-scored interviews, has no accommodation route, and allows employers to access other employers' private candidate records through changed IDs.

## Questions
1. **Title VII (25):** Explain intentional discrimination versus disparate impact under §2000e-2(a), (k), proof and defenses. Name missing data.
2. **ADA (20):** Analyze §12112(b)(5), (b)(6), (d) for test accessibility, alternatives and disability inquiries.
3. **Validation (25):** Design a controlled scientific validity/measurement study; explain why score authenticity is insufficient.
4. **Statistics (10):** Group A 40/100 selected, Group B 16/80. Calculate rates, impact ratio and limitations.
5. **Consent/security (10):** Create five cross-tenant/revocation synthetic tests and a credential status taxonomy.
6. **Claims (10):** Rewrite “scientifically validated, bias-free, objective” as a truthful pilot claim with explicit limits.

## Answer key
**1:** Section (a) concerns particular discriminatory treatment; §703(k) governs disparate-impact elements. Need role/job analysis, applicant demographics appropriate to law, outcomes, selection practice, causation, business necessity and alternatives. A disparate selection rate is a flag, not a final legal conclusion.

**2:** Investigate essential functions, reasonable accommodation and undue hardship, barriers in timed/voice/mouse-only testing, and inappropriate pre-offer disability/medical inquiries. Allow a privacy-respecting accommodation workflow and sufficiently validated alternative assessment.

**3:** Establish job competencies and test construct; independently sample relevant populations; pre-register hypothesis and scoring; estimate reliability, criterion evidence, subgroup behavior, accommodation equivalence, fairness and calibration uncertainty; audit score/rubric/model version changes.

**4:** A=40%, B=20%; ratio 20/40 = **0.50**. Below 80% heuristic, requires further analysis. The heuristic is not a statutory finding or sufficient statistical proof by itself.

**5:** Two-candidate/two-employer tests, current consent revision, role and tenancy guards, session revocation, concurrent grant withdrawal; separate self-reported, issuer authenticated, pilot/unvalidated, independently validated, suspended and revoked credentials.

**6:** Example: “This pilot assessment records authenticated responses and scores against rubric version X. It has not yet been validated to predict employment performance and should not be the sole basis for hiring decisions.” Only say “authenticated” where proven.

**Rubric:** statutory legal reasoning 30; evaluation science 25; controls 20; alternative explanations and limits 15; source citation accuracy 10.
