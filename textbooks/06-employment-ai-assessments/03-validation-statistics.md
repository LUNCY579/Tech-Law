# Chapter 3 — Selection validity, impact calculations and scientific gates

## 1. Four distinct validation claims
- **Authenticity:** recorded score came from the genuine test and signer.
- **Reliability:** score is sufficiently consistent under relevant conditions.
- **Construct validity:** instrument supports claims about the intended skill/trait.
- **Criterion/job-related validity:** evidence connects scores to relevant job performance or criteria, for the specific use and population.
None follows automatically from a correct digital signature.

## 2. Official selection guidance
The [EEOC's employee selection guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures) discusses the Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures (UGESP). The “four-fifths” or 80% selection-rate heuristic is **a screening rule of thumb, not a legal definition**. Small-sample statistical uncertainty and proper analytical methods matter. Read [EEOC's official UGESP Q&A](https://www.eeoc.gov/es/node/130157), which explicitly says the rule of thumb is not a legal definition.

## 3. Example (invented data)
Group A: 40 selected of 100 → 40%. Group B: 16 selected of 80 → 20%. Adverse-impact ratio (lower/higher) = 0.20 / 0.40 = **0.50**, below 0.80, flagging need for further investigation under the heuristic. It is **not by itself a court finding of discrimination**. Data quality, composition, job relevance, sample size and alternatives must be analyzed.

Reverse example: A 60% selection rate, B 51%, ratio 0.85. This passes the simple 80% heuristic, but does not prove lawful nondiscrimination; additional evidence can matter.

## 4. Selection science and governance
Pre-register instrument construct and intended use; map job analysis to item content; document administration consistency and accommodations; quantify measurement error; define acceptable distribution shifts, thresholds, calibration, subgroup tests, appeals and change controls. An internal test's high software unit-test coverage does not certify psychometric validity.

## 5. Repeated assessment integrity
Version exact question bank, rubric, scorer, model, reviewer and anti-cheating protocols. Independent human signoff for employment-use validity claims should differ from the issuer's own ability to sign a score. Store release gate evidence in a protected registry; an administrator flipping a boolean is not independent scientific validation.

## Exercise
**Q:** A company issues “top 1% engineer” badges based on a 20-question quiz of volunteer users. Is the percentile defensible for the general software labor market?  
**Answer:** Not without appropriate sampling, measurement, norming, test validity, construct and uncertainty evidence. The percentile may describe only the voluntary reference group; representing it broadly risks misleading claims.
