## Editorial distinctions and constraints

- Corporate textbook explains formation and stock mechanics but does not supply executed corporate papers or tax advice.
- SAFE calculations are **stated approximations or fully specified arithmetic examples**. No claim is made that a generic SAFE guarantees any ownership percentage.
- Reg D content is not an invitation to solicit investors without prior qualified securities review.
- For California, risk-assessment compliance may begin in 2026; certain automated decisionmaking obligations apply from **January 1, 2027** for covered users; revenue-dependent cybersecurity audit certifications have later dates. Do not collapse them.
- EU AI Act dates reflect official **2026 consolidated / summary** sources; always revisit operative amendments and transition conditions.
- First-party source links and original commentary are used; no model legal forms, complete agency PDFs or copyrighted financing templates are copied.
