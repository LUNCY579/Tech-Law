# Textbook I — Startup Corporate Law and Securities

**Version:** 2026-11-11 | **Audience:** U.S.-based technology founders | **Type:** Educational original commentary; not legal or tax advice.

This book starts with the legal entity and ends with a defensible financing process. It is a detailed teaching text, **not** a substitute for counsel reviewing signed financing, tax, corporate, or investor documents.

## Contents and sequence
1. [Entity design and governance](01-entity-governance.md) — formation, shares, boards, fiduciary duties.
2. [Founder equity, IP and vesting](02-founder-equity.md) — cap table, tax election, proprietary rights.
3. [SAFEs and dilution mathematics](03-safe-dilution.md) — conversion intuition and scenarios.
4. [Securities compliance and fundraising](04-fundraising-compliance.md) — exemption selection and solicitation.
5. [Applied examinations and model answers](05-assessment.md) — board approval, term sheet, fundraising, cap-table drills.

## What you should be able to do
- Distinguish **authorized**, **issued/outstanding**, **reserved**, and **fully diluted** shares.
- Identify which corporate actions require formal approvals under the governing documents.
- Explain why a SAFE's conversion result depends on the **actual legal instrument**, valuation definitions and other capitalization events.
- Recognize the difference between Section 4(a)(2), Rule 506(b), Rule 506(c), Regulation Crowdfunding and Rule 701.
- Track anti-fraud and notice-filing obligations without mistaking an exemption for immunity.
- Know which issues must go to startup and tax counsel **before** an action is taken.
