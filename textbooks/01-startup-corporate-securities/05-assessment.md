# Chapter 5 — Corporate and securities assessment

Write a legal memo, not a yes/no checklist. Cite exact statutory sections or official SEC guidance for every conclusion.

## Problem 1 — The unapproved founder issuance (25 points)
Three founders each hold 2M outstanding shares. The charter authorizes 7M total. The CEO unilaterally promises a fourth founder 2M more, citing an informal verbal agreement. What facts, approvals and legal documents are missing?

**Model answer:** Only 1M authorized-but-unissued shares remain on those facts; a 2M new-share issuance needs capital authorization analysis and potentially a charter amendment and required approvals. Also evaluate board/shareholder authority, consideration, employment/vesting, shareholder contractual rights, conflicts, securities exemption, ledger and tax. Nothing in the prompt establishes a valid issuance.

## Problem 2 — Restricted stock (20 points)
A founder receives restricted shares on March 3, then asks about an 83(b) election April 20.  
**Model answer:** Generally 30 days after property transfer under §83(b); on dates stated, the founder may have missed the election deadline. Exact transfer timing, weekends/holidays and eligibility require qualified review; neither mailing an arbitrary late form nor backdating is a lawful fix. [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f15620.pdf).

## Problem 3 — Public fundraising (25 points)
A founder with a planned Rule 506(b) round broadcasts on X: “Invest $10,000 for 1% of my startup.”  
**Model answer:** Public offering-style communications are a red flag for impermissible general solicitation in 506(b). Investigate whether any offer occurred, timing, audience, other exemptions, anti-fraud, and state requirements; obtain counsel before any additional communication. [SEC solicitation guidance](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/general-solicitation).

## Problem 4 — Dilution model (20 points)
Founders 7M, reserved pool 3M, and new financing gives investors 20% post-financing, assuming no other changes.  
**Model answer:** Pre-financing fully diluted 10M = 80%; post-financing 12.5M; investor shares 2.5M; founders 56%, pool 24%, investors 20%.

## Problem 5 — Distinguish source types (10 points)
Classify DGCL §141, SEC explanatory webpage, an executed SAFE template, and an attorney's advisory memo.  
**Model answer:** Statute; agency guidance; private contract; professional interpretation. Only the first is legislation, though other sources can have significant consequences.

### Graduation rubric
Accuracy and authority (30), issue identification (25), fact analysis (20), uncertainty and counterarguments (15), actionable evidence (10). A score of 85+ demonstrates good course mastery, **not professional qualification or legal compliance certification**.
