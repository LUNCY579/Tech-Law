# Textbook II — AI Regulation, Data Privacy and Automated Decisions

**Version:** 2026-11-11. Educational original text; not legal advice.

This text teaches **classification first**: what system, whose data, which jurisdiction, regulated role, intended use and effective date? No single “AI compliance certificate” covers all use cases.

### Primary official materials

- [CPPA completed rulemaking](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/ccpa_updates) and [CPPA timeline announcement](https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250923.html)
- [FTC Act §5](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45)
- [Title VII §703](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/2000e-2)

**Important temporal distinction:** The CPPA's rulemaking effective January 1, 2026 has later compliance dates for specified obligations. The EU AI Act has staged and amended dates. A provision's appearance in the text is not proof that the relevant obligation is already operative for a specific company.
