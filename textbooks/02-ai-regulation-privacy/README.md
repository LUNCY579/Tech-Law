# Textbook II — AI Regulation, Data Privacy and Automated Decisions

**Version:** 2026-11-11. Educational original text; not legal advice.

This text teaches **classification first**: what system, whose data, which jurisdiction, regulated role, intended use and effective date? No single “AI compliance certificate” covers all use cases.

### Milestone 17 cross-state consumer privacy expansion

The [Maryland MODPA and Oregon OCPA comparison](../../research/2026-11-13/08-maryland-oregon-privacy.md) explains differences in covered consumer counts, data-sale revenue thresholds, private employment-information exceptions, Maryland's distinct April 2026 prospectivity clause, and Oregon's 2026 privacy guidance. Complete the [implementation casebook](../../research/2026-11-13/09-state-privacy-implementation-exercises.md). These are **two targeted states**, not a national privacy survey. The separate [Milestone 19 Maryland 2026 amendment chapter](../../research/2026-11-13/11-maryland-modpa-2026-amendments.md) corrects 2024 §14-46xx citations to **current §14-47xx** and analyzes 2026 Ch874. Oregon's 2026 *direct-amendment index* gap was narrowly closed by [Milestone 18](../../research/2026-11-13/10-oregon-session-law-reconciliation.md); uncodified laws and judicial history remain open.

### State biometrics implementation (Milestone 21)

Read the [Washington RCW 19.375, Illinois BIPA and Texas §503.001 comparison](../../research/2026-11-13/15-washington-biometric-privacy.md). It explains why the same face or voice recognition service may have different legal outcomes depending on template enrollment, commercial versus security purpose, statutory exclusions, notice or release, and enforcement mechanism. This is source-based issue spotting, not legal approval.

- [CPPA completed rulemaking](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/ccpa_updates) and [CPPA timeline announcement](https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250923.html)
- [FTC Act §5](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45)
- [Title VII §703](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/2000e-2)

**Important temporal distinction:** The CPPA's rulemaking effective January 1, 2026 has later compliance dates for specified obligations. The EU AI Act has staged and amended dates. A provision's appearance in the text is not proof that the relevant obligation is already operative for a specific company.
