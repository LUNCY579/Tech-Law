## How to read the source labels

- **ENACTED_AND_APPLICABLE:** operative source for the stated scope/date, not a conclusion that a specific company is covered.
- **ENACTED_FUTURE:** enacted but stated substantive provisions begin later; verify exceptions and provisions effective earlier.
- **WITHDRAWN / VACATED:** historical agency or court material that must not be represented as operative authority.
- **OFFICIAL_GUIDANCE:** regulator explanation, useful but not equivalent to statutory text or binding case law.
- **REQUIRES_FURTHER_REVIEW:** official or draft source found, but exact legal conclusion, amendment, or trigger has not been confirmed.

**Research design:** state, actor role, product feature, legal trigger, exceptions, effective date, enforcement and current source must each be recorded. Never infer legal clearance from a state lacking a row in this sample.
