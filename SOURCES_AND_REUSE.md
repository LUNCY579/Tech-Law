# Source verification, citation and reuse policy

**Review date:** 2026-11-10. This project is educational and does not replace legal advice.

## Official sources used
| Jurisdiction/source | Official publisher | Citation form | Usage in this repo |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. Code | U.S. House, Office of Law Revision Counsel | Title U.S.C. § section | Links and original paraphrases, not bulk replication |
| U.S. Supreme Court opinions | Supreme Court of the United States | *Case*, volume U.S. page (year) | Case facts/holdings summarized, official PDF linked |
| Delaware General Corporation Law | State of Delaware | Del. Code tit. 8, § section | Short statutory explanation and link |
| California Consumer Privacy Act | California Legislative Information | Cal. Civ. Code §1798.100 et seq. | Applicability issue spotting and link |
| FTC orders and guidance | Federal Trade Commission | Matter name, file number, order date | Distinguish allegation, consent order and finding |
| SEC exemption guidance | U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission | Regulation D rule and statutory section | Context-specific explanation |
| AI Act / GDPR | EUR-Lex, Publications Office of EU | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689; Regulation (EU) 2016/679 | Explain operative articles, link originals |

## Editorial review gates
1. **Correctness:** Do not infer outcome from a headline. For opinions, read the controlling decision and check subsequent history; for statutes, check amendments/effective dates; for enforcement, distinguish allegations from adjudicated findings.
2. **Applicability:** Identify actor, jurisdiction, statutory threshold, exceptions and operative date.
3. **Pinpoint:** Cite actual sections, not only a home page. Use case reporter citations and court/year where verified.
4. **Quotation:** Prefer original explanatory paraphrase. Quote minimally with attribution; avoid copying full legal commentary.
5. **Re-use:** Public access and government domain names do **not** prove unrestricted licensing for all embedded content. U.S. federal government works may be public domain under 17 U.S.C. §105, subject to exceptions and third-party materials. Other jurisdictions have distinct copyright/re-use rules. Official EU materials may be subject to [EU reuse policies](https://commission.europa.eu/legal-notice_en). Linking is safer than mirroring entire texts. No broad license assertion is made about linked sources.
6. **Change control:** Date every substantive revision and re-verify regulatory changes.

## Case status mini-index
- *Google LLC v. Oracle America, Inc.*, 593 U.S. 1 (2021): **Supreme Court holding**, fair use on the specific copying; copyrightability assumed without being decided. [Opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/18-956_new_0e04.pdf).
- *Van Buren v. United States*, 593 U.S. 374 (2021): **Supreme Court holding**, interpretation of “exceeds authorized access” under CFAA. [Opinion](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/19-783_k53l.pdf).
- *In re Everalbum*, FTC File No. 192-3172: **FTC consent resolution**, not a litigated finding of liability. [Docket](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/192-3172-everalbum-inc-matter).
- *Specht v. Netscape Communications Corp.*, 306 F.3d 17 (2d Cir. 2002): **Second Circuit opinion** on notice/assent in software downloads; confirm an authoritative opinion copy and later citing history before relying on it.
- *Smith v. Van Gorkom*, 488 A.2d 858 (Del. 1985): **Delaware Supreme Court opinion**; read with later statutory developments, including DGCL §102(b)(7).

## Caution
Neither a source's location on an official website nor a publication's open access independently grants a transferable license to reproduce all of its content. We therefore keep the authored lesson material original, with concise factual commentary and outbound links. Course updates should be subjected to human source review.
