## Permissions policy
There is no defensible blanket statement that all material hosted by an official government site is unrestricted for republication. Original lecture prose, citation metadata and short attributed summaries are used. Do not import whole third-party texts, official seals, licensed charts or fonts by default. U.S. federal government works created by employees in official duties are generally excluded from U.S. copyright under 17 U.S.C. §105, but contractors, third-party content, non-U.S. government texts and websites can differ.
