## Reuse notes
Every page in this directory is **original explanatory text and brief source-linked summaries**. No entire judicial opinion, legal treatise, agency PDF, copyrighted artwork, or court seal was imported. U.S. official federal works are generally excluded from domestic copyright under 17 U.S.C. §105 subject to limitations, but state/EU documents and embedded third-party works may have other restrictions. Hyperlinks are not assertions of unrestricted license. For historic opinions we have not accessed an official copy, we deliberately provide no invented official URL.
