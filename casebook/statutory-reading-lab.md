# Statutory reading lab — element-by-element exercises

This is a research workbook, **not** an exhaustive treatise. Read the full current primary statute before applying a rule. A citation does not establish that a startup falls within a law's scope.

## I. FTC Act §5: fairness versus deception
**Primary:** [15 U.S.C. §45(a), (n)](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/45).  
**Reading order:** define “in or affecting commerce” → identify actor and conduct → separate deception (representation/omission and net impression) from unfairness → parse §45(n) substantial injury, reasonable avoidability and countervailing benefits → check specific industry statutes and defenses.  
**Exercise:** An AI startup advertises a “zero data retention” option although debugging records include excerpts. Separate likely representation issues from actual injury inquiries; identify what log samples, settings, contract text and user understanding you need.

## II. Contracts and arbitration
**Primary:** [9 U.S.C. §2](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/9/2) and [*Coinbase v. Suski*](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/23-3_879d.pdf).  
**Reading order:** prove agreement formation under applicable state law → identify transaction and FAA coverage → locate arbitration/delegation/venue text → examine conflicting agreements → test contract defenses and scope → identify who decides threshold disputes.  
**Exercise:** Does an enterprise MSA supersede a consumer promotional addendum? Show why precedence, parties and claim scope matter.

## III. Copyright and fair use
**Primary:** [17 U.S.C. §102](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/17/102), [§106](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/17/106), [§107](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/17/107); [*Google*](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/18-956_new_0e04.pdf), [*Warhol*](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-869_87ad.pdf).  
**Reading order:** classify protectable original expression → establish owner and exclusive right → identify copying and license → analyze each of the four factors in a specific use; weigh evidence without numerical threshold shortcuts.  
**Exercise:** Compare use of a small API declaration set for interoperability with copied commercial sprite art for a store listing. Explain why one case's result cannot be transplanted.

## IV. Securities and founder equity
**Primary:** [15 U.S.C. §77e](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/77e); [SEC exempt offerings](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/exempt-offerings); [Delaware DGCL §§101–102](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title8/c001/sc01/index.html).  
**Reading order:** corporate authorization/actual equity ownership → securities offer/sale → registration or exemption → investor category and solicitation restrictions → federal/state filings and anti-fraud disclosure.  
**Exercise:** Compare private accredited-investor conversations with a public crowdfunding landing page. Do not assume the same exemption applies.

## V. Data protection
**Primary:** [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj) Arts. 3, 5, 6, 12–23, 28, 32–34; [California CCPA regulations](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/).  
**Reading order:** jurisdictional and organizational scope → data/roles → processing basis → obligations and rights → exemptions → operational mechanism → timing and evidence.  
**Exercise:** A user requests deletion while a fraud investigation is open. Identify lawful exceptions and system-wide data inventory without declaring blanket automatic deletion.

## VI. Employment automation
**Primary:** [42 U.S.C. §2000e-2](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/42/2000e-2); [EU AI Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=OJ%3AL_202401689).  
**Reading order:** determine employer/developer/deployer roles → specific decision use → discriminatory treatment vs impact → EU scope and high-risk conditions → obligations and phased effective dates → records and independent review.  
**Exercise:** A U.S. hiring platform sells to an EU employer. Explain why both a jurisdictional scoping memo and a technical evaluation report are necessary.

## VII. Cybersecurity and computer misuse
**Primary:** [18 U.S.C. §1030](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/18/1030); [*Van Buren*](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/19-783_k53l.pdf).  
**Reading order:** access without authorization vs beyond authorization → statutory subsection and mental state → data category / loss/damage thresholds where relevant → other laws, contracts and remedial paths.  
**Exercise:** Compare use of legitimately visible records for a prohibited purpose against bypassing tenant permissions to view another customer's private files.

## Answer-writing standard
Every answer should have: (1) exact source and legal status, (2) issue, (3) statutory elements, (4) material facts / missing facts, (5) contrary interpretation, (6) narrow provisional conclusion, and (7) software evidence/control. Avoid “definitely legal” or “definitely illegal” where facts are absent.
