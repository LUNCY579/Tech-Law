## Corrections made

1. **Material:** current Maryland citations are **§14-47xx**, not the original **2024 §14-46xx** numbers. The 2026 bill cites a **2025 Replacement Volume** already bearing 47xx numbers; do not misattribute the recodification itself to the 2026 amendment.
2. **Material:** the earlier curriculum lacked **HB711/Ch874**: enacted 2026 inferred-sensitive data, geolocation and specified governmental sale/subpoena constraints.
3. **Clarification:** **2026 HB264 / Chapter 435** addresses state-government privacy and does not repeal the private-sector MODPA. It has distinct content from HB711/Ch874.
4. **Temporal:** the prior 2024 §14-4612 prospectivity receipt remains a historically correct citation, but **current citations use §14-4712**.
