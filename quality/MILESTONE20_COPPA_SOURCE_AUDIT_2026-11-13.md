# Milestone 20A — 2025 amended COPPA Rule source-currency audit

**As of November 13, 2026.** This is a **targeted enacted-rule audit** for a public teaching library, not an FTC approval, all-amendments audit, legal memorandum, licensed source republication or operational compliance certification.

## Official sources actually checked

1. **[FTC final rule, 90 Fed. Reg. 16918 (Apr. 22, 2025); 16 CFR part 312](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-04-22/html/2025-05904.htm)**. The official Federal Register **HTML** was examined for effective/compliance dates, revised definitions, parental consent, security, data retention and unadopted proposals. The rule states **June 23, 2025** effective date; most affected operators have **April 22, 2026** compliance date, with listed §312.11 safe-harbor exceptions.
2. **[FTC final-rule listing](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/16-cfr-part-312-coppa-final-rule-amendments)**. Confirms issuing agency and final-rule identity.
3. **[FTC announcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-finalizes-changes-childrens-privacy-rule-limiting-companies-ability-monetize-kids-data)**. Explanatory, not substitute binding text. Explains expanded biometric/government-ID personal information, separate disclosure consent and non-indefinite retention.
4. **[15 U.S.C. §6501](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/6501)**. Underlying statute reference, separate from the implementing rule.
