# Milestone 19B — Oregon SB 1516 / Chapter 77 source and scope audit

**Review date:** November 13, 2026. **Disposition:** targeted enacted-law and source-publisher verification; not a full state-law or compliance audit.

## Primary publisher evidence

- [Oregon Legislature, **Oregon Laws 2026, chapter 77**](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/lawsstatutes/2026orLaw0077.pdf), pp. **2–5**, visually inspected. Sections **3–9** define ALPR actors/covered information, permitted agency purposes, retention, limited sharing, vendor audit cadence, contract security, existing-contract treatment and narrow civil remedies.
- **§4(3), printed p. 2:** captured plate data not linked to court proceedings or ongoing investigation generally capped at **30 days**; investigative evidence and audit information separately treated.
- **§§6–7, printed pp. 3–4:** monthly and quarterly vendor audit, two-day publishing subject to redaction, agency procedures, contract requirements including agency ownership of data, end-to-end encryption and CJIS.
- **§§8–9, printed p. 5:** existing contracts may have transition, agency §4 compliance mandatory notwithstanding; vendor misuse limitations and specific causes of action.
- **§14, printed p. 11**, visually inspected: **emergency enactment; effective March 31, 2026**, not a projected future date.
- [Oregon Legislative Information System, SB 1516 enrolled overview](https://olis.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2026R1/Measures/Overview/SB1516) corroborates chapter **77** and describes law-enforcement ALPR scope. A staff digest is **not** binding where the enrolled statute says otherwise.

## Limitations and pending questions

- Chapter 77's ALPR provisions primarily regulate **law-enforcement-agency** use and **contracted ALPR vendors**, not every software developer.
- A 30-day default for certain ALPR records is **not** a blanket 30-day deletion clock for all information a software company holds.
- §6 audit publication requires applicable privacy redaction and does not authorize disclosure of raw private plate histories.
- Do not assert a commercial vendor is CJIS compliant, a contract is legally sufficient, or a deployment is lawful based solely on these explanatory notes.
- No independent 2026/2027 case citator, local-agency policy inventory or actual vendor-contract inspection was performed.
