## Corrections / editorial controls
- Uniformly distinguish a syllabus from a majority opinion, an FTC settlement from an adjudicated finding, an official publisher from a blanket redistribution license, and a commercial SaaS contract from a universally binding term.
- Do **not** certify case subsequent history as complete; no full citator service was used.
- Do **not** assert that a source “works” from an untested URL just because its syntax looks plausible.
- For official court opinions that are retrieved, keep precise **slip page** labels rather than implying printed reporter pages.

## Release conditions
Before using in actual investor outreach, employee screening or public Terms: consult relevant counsel, get the operative law version, compare executed documents, verify effective date and carry out product-specific compliance/testing.
