## Key corrections / omissions prevented

1. **Do not mix Illinois's 820 ILCS 42 Act** with its **775 ILCS 5/2-102(L)** employment discrimination rules: different triggers and controls.
2. **Do not call §42/20 universal**: demographic annual reporting has a **solely-AI** selection trigger.
3. **Do not claim an Illinois-only 30-day deadline for all AI data**: §42/15 is specific to video-interview recordings and applicant requests.
4. **Do not equate a NYC AEDT audit with proven hiring fairness or validity**: city rule and federal/state discrimination law are separate.
5. **Do not claim vendors necessarily bear NYC employer obligations**: DCWP identifies employer/employment agency as responsible, without resolving other vendor claims.
6. **Do not apply the 2027 Illinois amended section to 2026 facts**.
