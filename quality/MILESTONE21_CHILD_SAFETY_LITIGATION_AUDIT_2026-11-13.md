## Legislative source chain

- **Texas HB18:** [Texas Legislature, Business & Commerce Code Chapter 509](https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/BC/htm/BC.509.htm). Verified the statute's scope and exceptions in **§509.002**, distinct §509.057 coverage, and the 2024 operative date.
- **California AB2246, 2026 Chapter 187:** the [Governor's official September 10, 2026 signing announcement](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/10/governor-newsom-signs-the-strongest-child-safety-chatbot-and-social-media-laws-in-the-nation/) lists **AB2246**. A [complete chaptered copy on LegiScan](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/AB2246/id/3458541/California-2025-AB2246-Chaptered.html) was read and shows **repeal and reenactment** of the former Civil Code Title 1.81.47, including renumbered contents.
- **California Legislature viewer blocker:** both the [official bill page](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202520260AB2246) and official bill text URL returned **HTTP 403**. Thus **no issuing-legislature-hosted chaptered original was authenticated** here. This is a meaningful but bounded gap; do not call the LegiScan reproduction an official state-hosted source.
- **Effective date caveat:** the chaptered copy has **no urgency clause**, and California's generally effective date is **January 1, 2027** for ordinary 2026 statutes; recheck the exact final provision and official codification at any real product launch. Do not apply replacement Title numbering to the earlier Ninth Circuit litigation.
