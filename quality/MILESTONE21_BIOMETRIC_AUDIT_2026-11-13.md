## Material corrections in the new course

- Washington's **notice, consent, or prevention mechanism** is not an Illinois BIPA-style universal written-release requirement.
- Washington's covered purpose is narrowly defined; ordinary business possession, public video and security authentication need **additional factual classification**.
- RCW chapter 19.375 is **Attorney General enforced only**, unlike Illinois BIPA private action provisions.
- Retention requires purpose-specific analysis; no universal number of days.
- State definitions and exceptions may conflict, so the same embedding can trigger distinct analyses under different jurisdictions.
