## Source-status limitations

1. Maryland Ch. 454 official signed legislation was opened and its **p. 38** prospective clause visually verified. This distinguishes the law's Oct 2025 general effectiveness from the scope of §14-4612 relative to April 2026 processing.
2. Oregon's 2025 ORS chapter page warns about **2026 Chapter 646A amendments**, but the linked 2026 amendments-and-repeals table returned **403**. No comprehensive review of Oregon's 2026 changes was completed.
3. Maryland Ch. 435 (2026) regulates a different governmental subject from MODPA, despite its similarly worded title.
4. Neither regulator FAQ alone establishes every statutory requirement, current amendment history or litigation result.
5. Employment data, payments, nonprofits, motor vehicle manufacturers and regulated sectors may have distinct thresholds, exclusions and independent federal law.
